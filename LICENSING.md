# Software and data rights

The MIT license covers the original study software to the extent that Vedant Thaker holds the rights.

Study data use ARCHER2 allocation records from Jackson and Lamprakis, GB grid-intensity estimates from NESO and partners, and an ARCHER2 energy summary derived from Turner and Basden. See the [source attributions, original identifiers, modifications and licenses](study/README.md) for each input. The MIT license applies to author-owned software.
