# Primary-source register and comparison checks

1. Radovanović et al., [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPWRS.2022.3173250), [full author version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.11750). Introduction and Virtual Capacity Curves describe forecast-informed capacity controls for flexible workloads. They do not calibrate the present site or lookup energy.
2. Sukprasert et al., [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3627703.3650079), [author PDF](https://noman-bashir.github.io/assets/pdf/EuroSys-2024-Limitations.pdf). The abstract and workload analysis explain how attainable reductions vary with workload and regional traces.
3. Feng et al., [COAST v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05443v1), [primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.05443). Read sections III and IV-A/B. The 2011 CEA-Curie log supplies submissions, allocated cores and runtime. Jobs are filtered and partitioned into 224 daily windows; 165,887 jobs remain. The 67-window development set precedes held-out evaluation. The 2025 British series is counterfactually aligned at a random start per workload window. The assumed 10 W/core model and assigned deadlines/slack are documented. The held-out reported effect is 15.4% versus the earliest-feasible observed schedule, with 1.5 hours additional mean waiting. These are the source's model-based results.
4. Jackson and Lamprakis, [allocation data v1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14284189). Upstream Archer2SummaryData.csv SHA-256 d9487c2332fdca43ddae73d9f0640487c0c7587da3090d1c29c1273b9a0ca509. Selected positive-duration, positive-node records intersecting each week plus a two-hour margin. Rows are deduplicated when combining carbon data, not across distinct jobs.
5. Turner and Basden, [HPC-JEEP dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7128646). Upstream tar SHA-256 e18c5fd1d2d1790e2b47a82babe17d22e9c5166df10e74d7b9271967ab66ab95. Within ARCHER2/raw_data/2022-03_scua_sacct.dat.gz, positive measured joules, duration and nodes yield the included job-step power quantiles. Step identifiers cannot be linked to the anonymous allocations.
6. Turner and Basden, [HPC-JEEP technical report](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7137390). Section 3 and Table 1 pp. 7–9 document ARCHER2 meter coverage and its limitations.
7. National Energy System Operator and partners, [source and terms](https://carbonintensity.org.uk/). [First window](https://api.carbonintensity.org.uk/intensity/2022-03-05T00:00Z/2022-03-15T00:00Z), [second window](https://api.carbonintensity.org.uk/intensity/2022-03-12T00:00Z/2022-03-22T00:00Z), [additional drain](https://api.carbonintensity.org.uk/intensity/2022-03-21T00:00Z/2022-03-26T00:00Z). Inspected national actual series; matching overlap records are asserted. First and second raw JSON hashes c1c4f68601eaf8776d52016d9cb4ee462daad64fdd651c6e44541eb2a10cf1e4 and 67976f84661aaeea455c93d7c6ec345aa6a00e0d9dddead78b8e31c81595a7fc respectively. The API does not give original publication times with these estimates.
8. ARCHER2 Service, [hardware overview](https://docs.archer2.ac.uk/user-guide/hardware/). The official documentation gives 5,860 compute nodes for the full system. Service reservations and node unavailability cannot be inferred from this installed total.

## Why the earlier 2.6% value differs

The [earlier pinned replay](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/carbon-aware-scheduling-replay/tree/a31ba8a660c26067dddd8121c313332d9939a89b) tabulates 11,579.32 g divided by 448,312.13 g = 2.583% for week-one previous-day scoring with 60 minutes allowed delay. Its denominator covered 18,650 small and short eligible jobs, selected using realized duration, and the scoring rule evaluated each job's known full duration. The new forward rule uses the starting half-hour and the eligibility rule uses only node count and hypothetical participation. The new primary denominator is all jobs released that week. COAST uses a third, differently defined comparison.

The extended sample reuses source [4] in full and the meter calibration in [5]. For [7], the supplement records each fortnightly request and raw-response SHA-256 in `outputs/seasonal_inputs.csv` (December 2021 through mid-October 2022). The wider range supplies past same-weekday estimates and enough outcome intervals to cover completions. The study distributes weekly aggregates and retrieval code for these windows.
