# 04 — Reference and primary-source inspection

Numbering follows the revised manuscript. “Full” below describes the material actually consulted. It does not assert independent verification of the source authors’ other results.

| Ref | Source, access and locator | What the source supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Radovanović et al., [article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPWRS.2022.3173250), [full author PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.11750). Read abstract, introduction and description of Virtual Capacity Curves. | Google’s dispatch of temporally flexible work and forecast-informed capacity curves. It does not calibrate energy per candidate in our replay. |
| 2 | Sukprasert et al., [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3627703.3650079), [full author PDF](https://noman-bashir.github.io/assets/pdf/EuroSys-2024-Limitations.pdf). Abstract p. 924 and opening analysis describe the 123-region scope and properties of interactive and batch loads. | Limits on attainable average-carbon savings across workloads and grids. Does not certify the chosen ARCHER2 slack. |
| 3 | Feng et al., *COAST*, [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05443v1), [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.05443). Read §III scheduler and §IV-A data/energy, p. 4. | Uses a congestion-aware start search, CEA-Curie 2011 workload, energy and slack models, and 2025 UK grid intensity. Our historical-clock match and per-candidate overhead budget are narrower contributions. No priority or peer-review assertion. |
| 4 | Jackson and Lamprakis, [data release v1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14284189). Read release metadata, retrieved full Archer2SummaryData.csv, inspected header and processed both windows. | AllocNodes, Elapsed, Start, End and an opaque record ID. No submit or deadline column. Counts, candidate eligibility and replay statistics in the paper come from our extraction. |
| 5 | Turner and Basden, [HPC-JEEP data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7128646). Retrieved ZIP-independent full tar archive, read ARCHER2/raw_data/2022-03_scua_sacct.dat.gz, and computed quantiles from completed steps. | Positive node counts, runtimes and recorded joules give the empirical W/node distribution. The raw ID is a job-step identifier and there is no supported link to the anonymous record IDs in [4]. |
| 6 | Turner and Basden, [HPC-JEEP report release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7137390). Read full technical PDF, §3 and Table 1, pp. 7–9. Both authors are listed on the release record, whose order we use. | Documents energy monitoring, per-step accounting, missing readings and compute-node/system boundary. The paper uses hardware values as external power calibration. No scheduler meter was present here. |
| 7 | NESO and partners, [API, data documentation and license](https://carbonintensity.org.uk/). Inspected documentation, terms and the complete national-interval JSON returned by the dated API calls in DATA-SOURCES.md. | National average half-hourly intensity, actual estimate and forecast. Neither historical response contained a field giving forecast issuance time. The data provide average accounting rather than marginal avoided emissions. |
| 8 | Iosup and Jan, [Grid Workloads Archive GWA-T-2](https://atlarge-research.com/gwa-t-2/). Read full archive README.txt, fields Jobs and Jobs_Reservation, and queried the complete distributed Grid5000.sqlite database. | French multi-site OAR reservations. Some canceled reservations have stop −1, and some wait values can be negative. All included positive-runtime valid intervals in this extraction had zero spare time under a literal fixed-window interpretation. |
| 9 | Numenta, [actual AWS load-balancer count file](https://github.com/numenta/NAB/blob/master/data/realAWSCloudwatch/elb_request_count_8c0756.csv). Full 4,032-row series in distributed supplement, verified using the Git blob c2c83f9f470653d5db71f8aa6a038a98e075c7ae. | Observed counts and timestamps for the separate small-work-unit experiment. Neither job energy nor deferrability is recorded. |
| 10 | Lavin and Ahmad, [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICMLA.2015.141), [full author PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.03336), benchmark dataset description and introduction. | NAB distributed streaming time series to evaluate anomaly detection. It does not establish that network requests may wait. |

GraphOpticon, formerly cited for proactive autoscaling, was omitted because no autoscaling policy is evaluated in this revision. The empirical schedules and three comparator papers have distinct roles.
